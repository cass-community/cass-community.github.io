Join us at SC26 for activities across the conference program representing CASS and its member organizations.

The Supercomputing conference series is one of the leading destinations for the HPC scientific software community to gather and share their work and experiences, including many CASS members and software teams.

We are pleased to be a sizeable part of this year's edition, taking place 15-20 November 2026 in Chicago, Illinois, with a variety of tutorials, workshops, birds-of-a-feather sessions, and presentations.
