The User/Developer Experience (UDX) Working group hosts a regular discussion hour on UDX topics to complement our every-other-month webinars. 

This month, as a follow up to September's webinar on interviewing, we will talk about our experiences conducting formal interviews and having casual conversations with users. We'll share successful techniques we've tried, challenges we've encountered, and will address lingering questions from the webinar. Whether you've had good or bad luck learning about your product's user experience through interviews or informal chats, we want to hear from you!
